GOAL
Add the game-specific Valorant catalog and server-side pricing foundation while
preserving all existing Rocket League and global functionality.

VALORANT SERVICES
- Rank Boost
- Competitive Wins
- Placements Boost

RANK BOOST
- Current Rank -> Target Rank
- Maximum target: Immortal
- Duo: +100% of base service price
- RR Gain: 10 +20%, 20 0%, 30 0%, 45 -10%
- RR Amount: 0-20 0%, 21-40 -4%, 41-60 -5%, 61-80 -6%, 81-100 -8%
- Servers: Europe, North America, Brazil, Korea, SEA/OCE, Latin America
- Play Offline: free
- Agents Preferences: free
- Streaming: +$10
- Express Delivery: +30% of base
- +1 Extra Win: +$6
- Rank Insurance: +50% of base
- Percentage modifiers are additive and always based on the unmodified service base.

COMPETITIVE WINS
- Price per win by Current Rank
- 1 to 5 wins
- Solo / Duo
- RR Gain
- Server
- Same extras
- No RR Amount

PLACEMENTS BOOST
- Price per placement by Current Rank
- Includes Unrated
- 1 to 5 placements
- Solo / Duo
- Server
- Same extras
- No RR Gain or RR Amount

IMPORTANT
This phase establishes catalog + server pricing only.
It does NOT yet add the Valorant-specific configurator UI/rank badge assets.
Valorant should remain marked not-ready in the launch game list until the
configurator is completed in the next phase.
