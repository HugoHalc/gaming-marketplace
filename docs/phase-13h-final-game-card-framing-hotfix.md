# Phase 13H — Final Game Card Framing Hotfix

Purpose: final optical framing pass for the 7 Home game cards.

## What changed
Only per-game image framing values were refined.

- Rocket League:
  - slightly reduced scale
  - moved framing left to protect logo and give the car more breathing room

- League of Legends:
  - slightly reduced scale
  - adjusted vertical position for a more balanced character weight

- Valorant:
  - reduced scale
  - improved left-side breathing room and logo balance

- Marvel Rivals:
  - preserves the exact user-supplied logo asset
  - slightly reduced logo display size
  - smaller/lower-opacity watermark
  - visual subject scale reduced slightly

- Overwatch:
  - biggest scale reduction to prevent Genji from dominating the card
  - slightly higher framing balance

- Battlefield 6:
  - reduced subject dominance
  - moved framing left to better preserve the title/logo

- Rainbow Six Siege:
  - original user-provided card art and branding remain unchanged
  - only framing/scale adjusted
  - no new watermark or branding added
