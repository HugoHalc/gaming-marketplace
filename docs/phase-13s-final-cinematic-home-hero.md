# Phase 13S — Final Cinematic Home Hero

Final hero direction for BoostingPedia.

## Visual direction
Inspired by the strong cinematic presence of premium boosting marketplaces while preserving BoostingPedia's own identity and current Home structure.

## Changes
- Uses the final dark metallic BoostingPedia emblem artwork.
- Artwork is rendered as a large environmental layer rather than a card/panel.
- Larger right-side presence for a stronger premium gaming feel.
- Long, soft left-side mask keeps the text area clean.
- Emblem remains sharp and high-contrast.
- Artwork extends beyond the right side to avoid visible physical image boundaries.
- No decorative game panels, no fake UI, no extra text, and no noisy visual effects.

## Responsive
- Desktop: emblem dominates the right half with strong presence.
- Tablet: artwork remains visible without interfering with the copy.
- Mobile: artwork remains environmental and the text stays readable.

## Unchanged
- header
- navigation
- hero label
- headline
- paragraph
- buttons
- trust bullets
- hero height / general layout
- Home sections
- game cards
- Rocket League logic and pricing
