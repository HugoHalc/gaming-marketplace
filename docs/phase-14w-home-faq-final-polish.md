## Accordion visual polish
- Preserves two-column editorial layout.
- Preserves row-based accordion rather than cards.
- Subtle separators only.
- Questions use Geist semibold, 15–16px.
- Answers use Geist regular, #A0AAA4, generous line-height.
- Default icon circle remains dark and neutral.
- Hover lightly increases circle contrast and shifts the plus icon to light green.
- Open state uses a very dark emerald tint, ~30% emerald border, light-green icon.
- Plus rotates 45 degrees into a multiplication/cross shape.
- No glow, bounce, scale, heavy backgrounds or gamer decoration.
- 200ms contained transitions.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the transition.
